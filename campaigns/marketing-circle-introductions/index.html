WELCOME TO THE MARKETECTURE COMMUNITY

Use the comments below to introduce yourself and start a thoughtful marketing conversation. Share your name or role only if you are comfortable doing so.

Start with one question

  • Which Indian or global campaign made you stop and think recently — and why?
  • What marketing concept do you want explained through a real example?
  • What brand would you like the Marketecture team to decode next?
  • Are you working on a campaign, portfolio project or research idea and looking for constructive feedback?

Be respectful, stay on topic, credit original work and separate evidence from opinion. Do not post private information, spam or unverified accusations about people or organisations.
