Zomato: Conversational Humour & Push Notification Dominance

Standardised Case Study Architecture

INDIAN CAMPAIGN CASE STUDY • BRAND: ZOMATO • FORMAT: CRM & SOCIAL CONTENT

Zomato's marketing playbook transformed routine transactional app notifications and social media posts into an entertainment channel that millions of Indian smartphone users actively anticipate.

1. Brand Introduction

Founded in 2008 by Deepinder Goyal and Pankaj Chaddah, Zomato evolved from a restaurant discovery directory into a multi-billion dollar food delivery, quick commerce (Blinkit), and dining-out ecosystem across India.

2. Business Background & Challenge

In the fiercely competitive Indian food delivery duopoly with Swiggy, customer acquisition cost (CAC) and retention are daily battlegrounds. Traditional discount pushes suffer from severe banner blindness and diminishing returns.

3. Marketing Objectives

Drive daily active app engagement without relying solely on margin-eroding coupons, establish unaided top-of-mind brand salience during key meal-ordering hours, and transform users into organic brand advocates.

4. Target Audience

Urban Gen-Z and millennials aged 18–35 across Indian metros and Tier 1–2 cities who live fast-paced lives and treat food as an emotional coping mechanism and social experience.

5. Consumer Insight

Food is intensely emotional and tied to craving, guilt, boredom, and comfort. People do not want to be sold to; they want to laugh with friends over relatable food memories and mid-afternoon hunger pangs.

6. Campaign Idea & Platform

The Irreverent Foodie Companion: Position Zomato not as a corporate utility, but as an affectionate, slightly chaotic friend who understands your food cravings better than you do.

7. Creative Execution

Zomato transformed the mobile lock-screen into real estate for self-deprecating, witty micro-poetry: "Ghar ka khana is great, but biryani is an emotion." On Twitter/X and Instagram, they operate with topical memes, sarcastic public billboards, and founder-led transparency.

8. Advertising Message Hierarchy

Primary: Relatable food cravings. Secondary: Speed and reliability. Tertiary: Occasion-based prompts (rainy days, late-night snacking, cricket match nights).

9. Media & Distribution Strategy

Owned CRM mobile push notifications synchronized with contextual triggers (lunchtime at 12:45 PM, 3:30 PM tea break, 11:00 PM cravings) coupled with viral outdoor billboards and high-velocity social media memes.

10. Influencer & Community Involvement

Organic meme creators, comedian brand integrations (Ranveer Allahbadia, Samay Raina), and user-generated screenshots of notifications shared across LinkedIn and Twitter.

11. Verified Campaign Results

Reported over 15M monthly active transacting customers with customer acquisition costs significantly lower than traditional e-commerce benchmarks, driven largely by organic brand recall and lock-screen virality.

12. Key Marketing Lessons

  • Personality Over Promotion: A distinct brand voice turns push notifications from spam into content worth screenshotting.
  • Topical Agility: Real-time marketing requires autonomous social media teams empowered to publish without 7 layers of corporate approval.
  • Zero-Cost Virality: When notifications become memes, users distribute your brand message for free.
